You acknowledge that you understand the following:

- **Past performance does not predict future results.** All backtests are
  hypothetical, are computed with the benefit of hindsight, and do not
  represent trading in a real account. Real trading involves costs, taxes,
  liquidity constraints and behavioural factors the models do not capture.
- **Projections are illustrations, not forecasts.** They extrapolate historical
  return and volatility under simplifying assumptions (returns independent and
  identically distributed) that real markets violate.
- **The retirement outlook does not model your holdings.** It models the risk
  level you select, using an assumed relationship between volatility and
  expected return. Only the starting value comes from your portfolio.
- **News sentiment measures tone, not value.** It is a lexicon score over a
  small number of headlines with no established predictive relationship to
  future returns.
- **The AI assistant can be wrong.** Large language models produce confident,
  fluent, incorrect output. They can misread your portfolio and misstate

- **Market data may be wrong.** Data comes from third parties and may be
